Questions people usually have before trying it

CSVLink works like a visual PDF mail merge: your spreadsheet supplies the values, and your PDF supplies the layout.

Can I create PDFs from Excel?

Yes. Upload an Excel file, choose the columns you need, place them on the PDF, and generate one filled PDF per row.

Can I use my own PDF invoice template?

Yes. Upload your own invoice PDF and place customer, date, invoice number, items, tax, total, and other fields exactly where you want them.

Does it work with CSV files?

Yes. CSVLink supports CSV as well as Excel files, including exports from accounting tools, ecommerce platforms, CRMs, and custom spreadsheets.

Is this only for invoices?

No. Any repeatable PDF can work: receipts, certificates, packing slips, labels, letters, forms, reports, and more.
